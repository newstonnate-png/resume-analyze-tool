Data handling

How this private session handles your CV

Raw CV / resume upload

Your original PDF or DOCX is used only long enough to validate and extract the information needed for this private session. A successful intake is not reported until the temporary raw upload has been deleted.

Normalized Resume

The extracted structured Resume is kept only in the anonymous session so later analysis steps can work without retaining the original file. Session retention is intentionally temporary and is governed by the product's configured privacy lifecycle.

AI-assisted analysis

Later product stages may send extracted content to an approved AI-assisted analysis provider. Candidate content must not be sent to an unapproved third-party provider, and provider retention settings must satisfy the production privacy requirements before release.

Logs and analytics

Ordinary application logs and analytics use operational metadata such as anonymous session identifiers and error categories. They are not intended to contain your CV text, contact details, or proposed Resume wording.

Accounts

No permanent candidate account is required for the MVP. If the anonymous session expires, deleted personal content is not recoverable through the product.
